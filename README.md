# ASMVIL

Asmvil is a super lightweight deployment system.

It Helps you manage your servers, applications, and databases, all on your own hardware. Without the need for docker, kubernetes, or any other containerization technology. It is designed to be simple, fast, and easy to use.

Essentially cloud hosting but on your own hardware.

Oh and of course, free, opensource, and fully written in assembly.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Asmvil is in very early development, and in no way runnable or usable.  
> (With Saying that, I would also appreciate any feedback or contributions.)
>
